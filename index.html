About This ePortfolio

A professional collection of enhanced computer science work demonstrating my development as a software engineering professional.

I am completing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science with a concentration in Software Engineering. My career interests include software engineering, mobile application development, artificial intelligence, and the creation of secure, user-focused computing solutions.

The central artifact in this portfolio began as the Grazioso Salvare animal shelter dashboard from CS 340: Client/Server Development. The original application connected Python, Dash, Pandas, and MongoDB to help users identify dogs that might be suitable for rescue training.

During CS 499, I transformed the original dashboard into a modular Rescue Match Recommendation System. The three enhancements demonstrate software architecture, algorithmic problem-solving, database engineering, testing, security, documentation, and technical communication.
